# Email Writing

Write email that is clear, specific, and easy to act on.

## General Structure

1. Greeting
2. Context in one or two sentences
3. Main request or message
4. Details, bullets, or links
5. Clear next step
6. Closing

## Tone Modes

- Concise: short, direct, low ceremony.
- Professional: polished but not stiff.
- Friendly: warm and conversational.
- Formal: appropriate for executives, HR, legal, or official notices.
- Follow-up: references previous message and asks for the next step.
- Release / acceptance: summarizes changes, risks, evidence, and requested review.

## Language

- User-specified language wins.
- If no language is specified, match the user's language.
- Preserve names, product names, links, commands, and technical identifiers.
- For bilingual emails, keep sections clearly labeled.

## Quality Rules

- Put the most important point near the top.
- Use bullets for multiple asks or status items.
- Avoid vague phrases like "just checking in" unless the tone calls for it.
- Include dates, deadlines, versions, and links when relevant.
- Do not overstate claims that were not verified.
